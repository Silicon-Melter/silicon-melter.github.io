Noppawit Tharakittikirati

I'm teaching a simulated car to drive by watching an expert, using camera images and 3D LiDAR in a single neural network. I'm a third-year Robotics and AI Engineering student at KMITL in Bangkok, and I'm drawn to open questions like this one, where nobody knows the answer yet.

I started this research as a research intern with Prof. Jui-Chiu Chiang at National Chung Cheng University in Taiwan. Along the way I've designed a fused CUDA kernel, built a scheduler for the six GPUs I work on (the A100 and two RTX 4090s are sponsored by the AIMS Lab at KMITL), and published the driving datasets I collected.

Before that I built drones and robots. This site keeps the write-ups for both.
