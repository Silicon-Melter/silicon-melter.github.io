Hi, I'm Noppawit

I'm a third-year Robotics and AI Engineering student at KMITL in Bangkok. My focus areas are end-to-end autonomous driving, AI optimisation (custom CUDA kernels, inference engines, RAG, and context and prompt design), ML systems and agentic engineering.

My main project is end-to-end driving: one network that turns camera images and LiDAR into the path a car should drive. I started it as a research intern with Prof. Jui-Chiu Chiang at National Chung Cheng University in Taiwan. Along the way I've designed a fused CUDA kernel, built a scheduler for the six GPUs I work on (the A100 and two RTX 4090s are sponsored by the AIMS Lab at KMITL), and published the driving datasets I collected.

Before that I built drones and robots. This site keeps the write-ups for both.

Looking for an AI engineering role or a research internship. Thai (native), English (IELTS 7.5).
