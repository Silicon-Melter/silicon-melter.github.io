Noppawit Tharakittikirati

I'm a third-year Robotics and AI Engineering student at KMITL in Bangkok. I enjoy making deep learning models run faster, and then measuring whether they really did.

Most of my time goes into end-to-end autonomous driving: one neural network that takes in camera images, and in our case LiDAR, and outputs the path the car should drive. I started this as a research intern with Prof. Jui-Chiu Chiang at National Chung Cheng University in Taiwan. Along the way I've designed a fused CUDA kernel, built a scheduler for the six GPUs I work on (the A100 and two RTX 4090s are sponsored by the AIMS Lab at KMITL), and published the driving datasets I collected.

Before that I built drones and robots. This site keeps the write-ups for both.
