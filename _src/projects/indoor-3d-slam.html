Indoor 3D SLAM with an RGB camera and 2D LiDAR

2025 – 2026 · Python, ROS 2 Jazzy, RTAB-Map, MAVROS, OpenCV, MiDaS, EKF, Raspberry Pi, UWB

3D LiDARs make indoor mapping easy, but they are expensive and heavy for a small drone. This project built a 3D map from a cheap 2D laser scanner and an ordinary camera instead.

Add a photo of the drone or a screenshot of a map here.

How it works

  • Pseudo-depth. MiDaS estimates depth from a single RGB image, but only up to an unknown scale. The 2D laser scan gives true distances along one plane. Projecting the scan onto the image anchors the MiDaS depth to real metres, so the camera behaves like an RGB-D sensor.
  • Mapping. RTAB-Map builds the 3D map from the pseudo-RGB-D stream.
  • Position without GPS. Visual-inertial odometry combines optical flow and the IMU through MAVROS, fused with an EKF.

Testing

The drone was flown in the Ultra-Wideband (UWB) testbed at Temasek Laboratories, National University of Singapore, which gives a ground-truth position to compare the estimate against.
